Blowdown labeling

How to label

Judge only the 30 m cell inside the magenta square. Each image is 1 km across. The left panel is before the storm and the middle panel is after; some cells also show an aerial photo taken after the storm.

Damaged: the canopy in the cell is visibly broken, thinned or flattened after the storm (brown or grey where it was green, downed-tree texture in the aerial photo). Intact: the canopy looks the same before and after. Unsure: you can't tell (clouds, haze, flooding, the cell is not forest, or the change is ambiguous).

Labels are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back. Use Download labels.csv when you're done (or any time, as a backup) and send that file back. Clearing the browser's site data deletes the saved labels.
